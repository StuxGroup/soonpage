Cookies Policy

Short answer: we don't use cookies. Here's the long answer.

Effective: September 2026

No tracking cookies

This page sets no cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics of any kind. There are no analytics scripts, no ad networks, and no tracking pixels anywhere on it.

What we actually store

The only browser storage used here is localStorage — distinct from cookies, and never sent to any server the way a cookie is. It holds exactly one thing:

  • sg-theme — remembers whether you last chose light or dark mode, so you don't have to reselect it on your next visit.

That's stored entirely on your device and can be cleared any time via your browser's site data settings — clearing it just resets the page to its default theme.

GitHub Pages

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may set its own cookies or perform its own logging as part of serving the page, outside of anything Stux.Group controls — see GitHub's privacy statement for that.

Consent management

There's no cookie consent banner here because nothing on this page legally requires one — there are no non-essential cookies to ask permission for.
