Privacy Policy

Short version: this page collects nothing. Here's the long version anyway.

Effective: September 2026

The short version

This is a plain static HTML "coming soon" page for an upcoming Stux.Group project, hosted on GitHub Pages. There's no server-side code, no database, no account system, and no forms of any kind — so there's nothing for us to collect.

This website

There is no email signup, newsletter, contact form, or any other way to submit information on this page. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what's collected and why.

The page does load a few resources directly from third-party CDNs, straight from your browser:

  • The Barlow font, from Google Fonts.
  • Font Awesome icons, from Cloudflare's cdnjs.
  • The logo and icon images, from global.media.stux.group.

Those requests go straight from your browser to Google's, Cloudflare's, and our own media host's servers — we don't see them, and each provider handles them under its own policy.

localStorage

The only thing this page stores in your browser is a single value, sg-theme, remembering whether you last chose light or dark mode. It never leaves your device, isn't sent to any server, and can be cleared any time via your browser's site data settings.

Links to other sites

The social icons on this page link out to our Bluesky and LinkedIn profiles. Those are third-party platforms with their own privacy policies — we don't control what they collect once you click through.

Children's privacy

This page isn't directed at children, but since it collects no personal information from anyone, there's nothing specific to say beyond that.

Changes to this policy

If this page grows features that collect data (an email signup, for instance), this policy will be updated first to reflect exactly what's collected and why. The date above marks the most recent revision.

Questions about privacy? Email legal@stux.group.
