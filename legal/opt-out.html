Opt-Out Preferences

The short version: there's nothing to opt out of.

No data sale or sharing

Laws like California's CCPA/CPRA give you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data. This page has nothing to opt out of in that sense — it collects no personal information for advertising or analytics, so there's none to sell or share.

What actually happens

Fonts and icons are now served from this same domain rather than a third-party CDN. The only outside data flow is your own browser fetching the logo and icon images from our own media host (see the Privacy Policy) — not something we could sell even if we wanted to; we're not in the middle of the request.

Do Not Track

Because this page runs no tracking scripts, a browser's "Do Not Track" signal has no additional effect here — there's nothing being tracked either way.
